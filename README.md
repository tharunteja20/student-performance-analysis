# Student Performance Analysis

This is a simple project where I analyzed a dataset of 1000 students 
to see how things like test preparation and parental education level 
affect math scores.

## What I did
- Loaded the dataset and checked its size, columns, and if there were 
  any missing values or duplicates
- Grouped students based on whether they completed test preparation 
  and compared their average math scores
- Grouped students by their parents' education level and compared 
  average math scores there too
- Made a bar chart and a histogram to visualize the results

## What I found
- Students who completed the test prep course scored higher on 
  average than students who didn't
- Students whose parents had higher education levels tended to score 
  a bit higher on average too
- Most students scored somewhere between 50 and 80, and the scores 
  followed a roughly normal (bell-shaped) distribution

## Tools I used
Python, pandas, matplotlib
